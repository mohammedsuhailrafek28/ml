# Presentation talking points

1. **Title** — Medical AI Suite is an educational multi-disease ML system.
2. **Problem** — Public health datasets require careful cleaning and leakage controls.
3. **Objective** — Compare seven models and provide cautious dataset-pattern model outputs.
4. **Five diseases** — Each module uses a shared, reproducible architecture.
5. **Architecture** — Validation, cleaning, pipelines, selection, persistence, UI.
6. **Datasets** — UCI ILPD, Cleveland Heart, Pima Diabetes, UCI CKD, UCI Parkinsons.
7. **Cleaning** — Missing values and coded categories are documented, not hidden.
8. **Preprocessing** — Fit only on training data to prevent leakage.
9. **Models** — Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, and CV stability guide selection.
10. **Results** — Metrics are dataset-specific and not clinical evidence.
11. **Parkinsons leakage** — Subject grouping substantially changed evaluation.
12. **Streamlit** — Users enter measurements and receive cautious model output.
13. **Explainability** — Global permutation/native importance is not causation.
14. **Ethics** — No diagnosis, treatment, or clinical deployment claims.
15. **Conclusion** — Useful university workflow; external clinical validation remains essential.
